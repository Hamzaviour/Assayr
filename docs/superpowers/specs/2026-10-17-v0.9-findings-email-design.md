# v0.9: Audit findings with corrective actions, and email reminders — design

Status: draft for approval (answers 2026-10-17: findings come from audit results; ISO-style grades;
full CAPA with independent verification; mail server set by an administrator, one daily digest per
person).

## Goal

Close the loop after an audit: every fail, partial or SOC 2 exception can become a finding with an
owner, a root cause and corrective actions that are tracked to closure with evidence and checked for
effectiveness by someone else. Then make sure nobody misses a due date: a daily email, sent through
the organisation's own mail server, tells each person what is due or overdue for them across Assayr.

## Scope

In: findings raised from internal audit results (ISO 27001, NIA, ISO 42001, SOC 2); grades; root cause,
correction and corrective actions; evidence files on actions; verification of effectiveness; raising a
finding as a risk; findings register with Excel export; dashboard card; mail server settings with a
test email; a daily digest per person covering findings, actions and the existing due dates in
Assayr; a per-person opt-out.

Out (later): findings from external/certification audits and other sources (the model keeps a
`source` column so they can be added without a migration of existing rows); automatic findings from
breaches or DPIAs; per-item emails; email in other languages; Microsoft 365/Gmail OAuth sign-in for
SMTP (basic SMTP with a username and password, or no authentication, only).

## 1. Findings (`assayr.findings`)

### Model

- `finding`: `id`, `scope_id`, `code` (`F-001`, per scope, from `seq`), `seq`, `title` (≤ 300),
  `description` (text), `grade`, `source` (`internal_audit` in v0.9), `audit_id` (nullable FK,
  `SET NULL`), `audit_item_id` (nullable FK, `SET NULL`, **unique** when not null: one finding per
  audit row), `framework` and `ref` (copied from the audit and row, so the finding still reads
  correctly if the row is removed), `owner_id`, `due_date`, `status`, `root_cause`, `correction`
  (the immediate fix), `response` (for observations and OFIs), `raised_by_id`, `raised_at`,
  `verifier_id`, `verified_at`, `verification_note`, `closed_at`, `risk_id` (nullable FK to the
  risk register), timestamps.
- `corrective_action`: `id`, `finding_id` (cascade), `seq` (1, 2, … within the finding), `description`,
  `owner_id`, `due_date`, `status` (`open`, `done`), `done_at`, `done_note`.
- `corrective_action_file`: `id`, `action_id` (cascade), `sha256`, `filename`, `size`, `mime`,
  `uploaded_by_id`, `uploaded_at`. Files go through the same store, size limit and ClamAV scan as
  policy and evidence files.
- Migration `0013_findings_mail.py` (with the mail tables below).

### Grades

`major` (Major nonconformity), `minor` (Minor nonconformity), `observation` (Observation), `ofi`
(Opportunity for improvement).

Default when raised from an audit row (the auditor can change it):

| Audit result | Default grade | Default due date |
|---|---|---|
| fail | major | raised + 30 days |
| partial | minor | raised + 90 days |
| SOC 2 exception | minor | raised + 90 days |

Observations and OFIs default to raised + 180 days. Due dates are always editable.

### Lifecycle

Major and minor (nonconformities):

`open` → `action_planned` → `awaiting_verification` → `closed`

- `open`: raised; needs an owner, a root cause and at least one corrective action.
- `action_planned`: root cause recorded and at least one action exists. Entered automatically.
- `awaiting_verification`: every action is `done`, each with a note or at least one file. Entered
  automatically when the last action is marked done.
- Verification by a **second person**: someone with `findings:verify` who is not the finding owner
  and not the owner of any of its actions. Outcome:
  - effective → `closed` (`verified_at`, `verifier_id`, `verification_note`, `closed_at`);
  - not effective → back to `action_planned` with the note; the owner adds or reopens actions.
- A closed finding can be reopened by an ISMS manager with a reason (back to `action_planned`).

Observations and OFIs: `open` → `closed`. The owner closes it with a `response` (what was done, or
why no action is taken). No root cause, actions or verification required (actions are allowed).

Every change is written to the audit trail.

### Raising findings from audits

- Audit detail: rows with fail, partial or exception show **Raise finding** (or the finding code with
  its status badge once raised). The form is prefilled: title "`<ref>`: `<result>`" plus the row's
  notes or exception note as description, grade and due date from the table above, owner = the
  control's owner if it has one.
- On **Complete audit**, if any fail/partial/exception rows have no finding, the page offers
  **Raise N findings** (one click, defaults as above; the response lists what was created).
- `POST /audits/{id}/findings` with `{"item_ids": [...]}` or `{"all": true}`: creates findings for
  the rows that do not have one yet; idempotent (the unique `audit_item_id` guarantees it under
  concurrency). Rows whose result is not fail/partial/exception are rejected with 422.
- Reopening or changing an audit never deletes findings. If a row's result changes to pass/no
  exception after a finding exists, the finding stays and the audit row shows "result changed since
  the finding was raised".

### Raise as a risk

Like AI impacts: **Raise as risk** on a finding adds it to the risk register once (title
"Finding `F-001`: `<title>`", category "Audit finding", owner = finding owner); raising again returns
the same risk. The finding shows a link to it.

### Permissions

- New `findings:manage`: `isms_manager`, `internal_auditor`. Raise, edit any field, assign owners,
  add actions, reopen (reopen: `isms_manager` only, as for audits).
- New `findings:verify`: `isms_manager`, `internal_auditor`.
- Finding owners: edit root cause, correction, response, actions on their findings; close
  observations/OFIs.
- Action owners: mark their action done, add notes and files.
- Everyone with `controls:read`: read.

### API

`/findings` (list with filters: status, grade, owner=me|none|id, overdue, audit_id, framework; sort by
due date), `/findings/{id}` (detail with actions, files, history), `POST/PATCH` finding,
`POST /findings/{id}/actions`, `PATCH /findings/{id}/actions/{aid}`, `POST .../files`,
`GET .../files/{fid}`, `POST /findings/{id}/verify` (`{"effective": bool, "note": str}`),
`POST /findings/{id}/close` (observations/OFIs), `POST /findings/{id}/reopen`,
`POST /findings/{id}/raise-risk`, `GET /findings/export.xlsx`, `GET /findings/summary` (counts by
status and grade, overdue).

## 2. Email (`assayr.mail`)

### Settings

- `mail_settings` (one row): `enabled`, `host`, `port`, `security` (`starttls`, `ssl`, `none`),
  `username`, `password_encrypted`, `from_address`, `from_name` (default the organisation name),
  `base_url` (the address people use to open Assayr, for links in emails), `digest_hour` (0–23,
  server local time, default 7), `updated_by_id`, `updated_at`.
- The password is **write-only** in the API (never returned; the form shows "saved" and a Change
  button), never written to the audit trail, and encrypted at rest with Fernet using
  `ASSAYR_SECRET_KEY` from `assayr.env`.
  - Fresh installs: both installers generate the key.
  - Upgrades: the Windows provisioning step and the Linux `upgrade` command add a generated key when
    `assayr.env` has none (existing values untouched).
  - Backups already include `assayr.env`, so a restore keeps the key. If the key is missing at run
    time, saving a password is refused with a clear message and sending is skipped with a logged
    reason.
- Page **Settings → Email** for `settings:manage` (platform administrator): the fields above, **Send
  test email** (to the administrator's own address, synchronous, shows the server's error text on
  failure), and the last digest run ("sent to 14 people at 07:00 · 1 failed: …").
- Email is off until an administrator enables it. This is Assayr's only outbound connection, and only
  to the mail server the organisation sets. The README's security section says so.

### Daily digest

- The worker already runs scheduled sweeps; it adds a `mail_digest` job each day at `digest_hour`.
- `mail_log`: `id`, `user_id`, `kind` (`digest`, `test`), `day` (date), `status` (`sent`, `failed`,
  `skipped`), `error`, `item_count`, `created_at`; **unique** (`user_id`, `kind`, `day`) for digests,
  so restarts or two workers never send twice.
- One email per active person with at least one item; nothing is sent to someone with nothing due.
  Plain text plus a simple HTML version, subject "Assayr: 3 overdue, 5 due this week".
- Contents (each line links to the item using `base_url`), grouped Overdue / Due in the next 7 days /
  Coming up (next 30 days, for expiry and reviews):
  - findings they own (open, not closed) by due date; corrective actions they own; findings waiting
    for their verification (people with `findings:verify`, when any finding is
    `awaiting_verification` and they are eligible);
  - evidence expiring on controls they own (`artefact_link.expires_on`);
  - policies, procedures and guidelines they own with `review_due`;
  - training assignments they must complete (`due_at`);
  - risks they own with `treatment_due` or `review_due`;
  - privacy officers: data subject requests by deadline, breaches with a notification deadline in the
    next 72 hours, DPIAs and processing activities with `review_due`;
  - AI system owners: impact assessments with `review_due`.
- Per-person opt-out: "Email me a daily summary" on the person's own profile menu (default on);
  administrators can see but not change it.
- Sending uses Python's `smtplib` with a 30 s timeout; a failure for one person is logged and does
  not stop the others; a server-level failure (cannot connect, authentication) marks the run failed
  once and is shown on the settings page.

## 3. UI

- Navigation: **Findings** under Assure, after Audits (everyone with `controls:read`).
- Findings page: status bar (shared `Status` registry, new areas `finding`, `findingGrade`,
  `action`), filters (status, grade, owner, overdue, framework), table with code, title, grade badge,
  ref (links to the control or NIA control), owner, due date (red when overdue), status; Excel
  export.
- Finding detail: header (code, grade, status, due date, owner, source audit link, risk link);
  sections Description · Root cause and correction · Corrective actions (each with owner, due date,
  done toggle, note, files) · Verification (form for eligible verifiers; history of verdicts) ·
  Response (observations/OFIs). Actions the current person can take are the only buttons shown.
- Audit detail: finding chip or **Raise finding** per eligible row; **Raise N findings** after
  completion.
- Dashboard: Monitoring card **Findings** (open by grade, overdue count, link to the register
  filtered to overdue).
- Settings → Email page; profile menu toggle for the digest.

Status registry:

| Area | Values |
|---|---|
| `finding` | open (danger "Open"), action_planned (progress "Action planned"), awaiting_verification (review "Awaiting verification"), closed (success "Closed") |
| `findingGrade` | major (danger "Major NC"), minor (warning "Minor NC"), observation (neutral "Observation"), ofi (muted "OFI") |
| `action` | open (neutral "Open"), done (success "Done"); overdue shown as warning "Overdue" |

## 4. Error handling

- Raising a finding for a pass/NA/no-exception/not-tested row: 422 "Only fail, partial or exception
  results can be raised as findings."
- Raising twice for the same row: returns the existing finding (200, not a duplicate).
- Verifying as the finding owner or an action owner: 403 "Someone who did not own this finding or its
  actions must verify it."
- Verifying before every action is done: 409 "N corrective actions are still open."
- Marking an action done with neither a note nor a file: 422 "Add a note or a file showing what was
  done."
- Closing a nonconformity through `/close`: 409 "Major and minor nonconformities close through
  verification."
- Mail: test email failure shows the SMTP error text; saving with `security=none` on port 465 or
  `ssl` on 587 shows a hint, not an error.
